# Generated document safety

## [DOC-01] Internal case briefs only
Generated documents in this demo are synthetic internal case briefs for testing and agent review. They must be labeled DEMO ONLY and SYNTHETIC. They are not invoices, receipts, refund confirmations, statements, identity records, or evidence of a real transaction.

## [DOC-02] Never fabricate transaction evidence
Do not invent payment references, bank confirmations, refund amounts, dates of transfer, or official company marks. When a customer asks for proof of payment or a refund, direct staff to verify and retrieve the real record from an approved payment or finance system. The demo backend has no payment-system integration.
